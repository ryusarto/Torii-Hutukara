# Gestos ambientais do Capítulo 3 — vida-v2

24 poses bitmap novas (quatro personagens × seis poses), geradas pelo ImageGen integrado com alpha real. As quatro referências são os atlas originais em `assets/chapter-03/runtime/personagens`, registrados por linha no manifesto. Preservam personagens, roupas, pele, cabelo e utensílios.

- Fonte: `C3_GESTOS_FONTE.png`, saída ImageGen `exec-15e9cb98-4293-4b5e-b03b-b5e9d79cf083.png`.
- Atlas: `NPC_C3_GESTURES_6X4.png`, células256×256, pés(128,256).
- Processamento: `production/prepare_npc_c3_gestures.py`; recorte, escala de altura e empacotamento, sem desenhar poses por código nem interpolar frames. Preserve o original.
- Cada linha tem seis hashes diferentes. A IA local controla olhar, pausa, trabalho, gesto e passeio curto; o atlas frontal entra quando parado, e a arte direcional existente continua durante movimento/virada. O relógio congela em menus/diálogos/foco perdido. Nenhum NPC altera progressão ou posição de um evento da história.
- O render direcional corrige a proporção nativa68×181 (a largura fixa anterior68×82 achatava o personagem).

## Prompt usado

References each show four view directions; use only the FRONT-facing identity to create brand-new subtle acting poses. Output one sheet, ideally 1536x1024, strict 6 columns by 4 rows, each cell equal size. NO text, labels, grids, backgrounds or cast shadows. True alpha transparency between sprites. Each entire character fits inside its own cell, centered, identical scale and feet on one consistent baseline across all 6 cells in its row. No overlapping adjacent cells. Crisp detailed pixel art matching the reference, no blur. Row 1 reference 1: young dark-skinned woman with twin afro puffs and magenta jacket, turquoise trousers and shoulder bag. Row 2 reference 2: curly-haired dark-skinned young man with turquoise band, magenta/turquoise coat. Row 3 reference 3: woman canal mechanic with purple cap and turquoise/ochre workwear. Row 4 reference 4: man industrial operator with raised amber goggles and workwear. Exactly six distinct sequential frames per row, all facing FRONT: (1) quiet neutral breathing posture, (2) glance slightly aside with one hand beginning to rise, (3) natural blink while raising hand, (4) small meaningful gesture: row1 adjust bag strap, row2 check a handheld music device, row3 inspect a small tool with one hand, row4 adjust goggles with one hand, (5) hand lowering toward neutral with open eyes, (6) shoulders and hands settled toward the first pose for a seamless loop. Preserve face, hairstyle, clothing, skin tone and each character's relative body proportions in EVERY frame. Legs stay planted with no walking-in-place, no bobbing or rotating the entire sprite. Movement is localized to arms, head, eyes, breathing. No new accessories except the small held tool/device already implied by their role. Maintain the game's existing stylized top-down 3/4 RPG view and exact pixel density.
