### faces

Use case: identity-preserve. Create a refined character face and character reference board for the TWO MAIN heroes of Torii & Hutukara, NOT the percussionist or synthesizer variants. Reference 1 is Ryuchi's real face and BLACK FITTED FABRIC CAP; reference 2 is Ryuchi's existing in-game sprite costume; reference 3 is Franco's existing in-game sprite costume; reference 4 is Franco's real face. Preserve recognizable facial identity and original outfits/colors. Ryuchi must clearly wear a close-fitting black folded-brim fabric cap/beanie, with visible cloth seam and cuff, covering all forehead hair, NO bangs, NO hair shaped like a fringe, no long trailing bandana tails; short dark sideburns only. Face: strong dark eyebrows, medium light warm skin, subtle moustache and chin stubble as real photograph. Franco: brown skin, dark curly voluminous hair, full lips, strong eyebrows, subtle moustache and short chin beard matching photo, natural attractive face, not generic anime. Art: exceptionally clean refined 16-bit JRPG pixel art character design, carefully placed pixels, readable facial shapes, no blur, no photo texture. Layout TWO horizontal rows; RYUCHI top, FRANCO bottom. Each row has THREE large detailed head-and-shoulder portraits FRONT, THREE-QUARTER, PROFILE followed by ONE smaller full-body front-facing sprite matching existing proportions and clothing. Consistent features in all angles. Labels RYUCHI and FRANCO only. Neutral expressions for modeling, not speaking. No masks on faces, no weapons, no new costumes. Real PNG transparent background, isolated figures, no panels/checkerboard drawn. Provide high-resolution review asset, not a game-ready animation atlas; preserve detailed face geometry.

### ryuchi

Use case: stylized-concept. Pixel art equipment sprite sheet for RYUCHI MAIN CHARACTER from Torii & Hutukara. MATCH THE PIXEL ART TEXTURE of the attached original character sprite, including its chunky visible square pixels, dark pixel outlines, restricted color ramps and stepped shadow clusters. This is an actual 16-bit JRPG pixel-art clothing board, NOT a photograph, NOT a 3D render, NOT fabric photography. All garment detail must be drawn as intentional pixel clusters; no smooth texture, no smooth gradients, no blur, no realistic weave, no antialiasing. Uniform pixel grid and nearest-neighbor appearance across all items. Refer to original sprite for costume colors and the portrait board only for outfit consistency. Clothing ONLY, no human body, no heads. Each clothing item SEPARATED and isolated, no overlapping. FIVE rows: 1 fitted BLACK FABRIC TOUQUINHA with a clearly folded cuff, seam and white zigzag embroidery, no hair or bangs. 2 cobalt blue jacket with pale chevron sleeve/back trim, navy cuffs and cream collar piping. 3 white collared shirt and separately isolated red and gold necktie. 4 dark plum trousers. 5 black ankle boots. Three columns: FRONT, SIDE, BACK; consistent shape and scale for the same item across all angles. Each item should read as a 48-80 pixel tall game equipment sprite enlarged with crisp square pixels. Clothing arranged on invisible mannequins but entirely pixel-drawn, preserving empty neck openings. Clear gutters. Labels in small pixel lettering: FRENTE, LATERAL, COSTAS and garment names in Portuguese. PNG with REAL FULLY TRANSPARENT ALPHA background between every item; no background color, NO BACKGROUND GRADIENT, no glow, no floor or cast shadows, no baked checkerboard. No realistic materials. Priority: unmistakable 16-bit sprite texture like the main character. The beanie is CLOTH, not a hairpiece.
